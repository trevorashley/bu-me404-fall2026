# Class Activities

Follow each activity's directions for individual or partner work. Develop the construction by hand, review the reasoning during class discussion, and compare it with MATLAB.

| Activity | Topics | Class time |
|---|---|---|
| [Root Locus, One Rule at a Time](class-activities/root-locus-five-rules.md) | Individual guided practice: six problems with progressive landscape plots, pole snapshots, gain selection, and a retrieval check | About 145 minutes over two meetings |
| [Root locus: read, predict, and check](class-activities/root-locus-gain-design.md) | Individual guided practice: six rounds with two-minute checkpoints, fill-in-the-blank landscape worksheets, and instructor MATLAB checks | 105 minutes (one lecture) |
